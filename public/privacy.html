Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 6, 2026

Loop ("we," "us," "the app") is a voice- and text-capture tool that turns what you say or type into organized tasks, calendar events, and notes. This policy explains what information we collect, why, and how it's handled.

What we collect

  • Your email address — used only to log you in (a one-time code, no password).
  • What you capture — the voice transcripts and typed notes you submit, and the tasks/events/notes Loop extracts from them.
  • A personal profile — short, durable observations Loop builds over time about how you work and like to be reminded (e.g. "prefers studying in the morning"), used only to personalize your own experience.
  • Google Calendar data — only if you choose to connect it: we request permission to create events on your primary calendar. We do not read your existing calendar, contacts, or any other Google data.
  • Basic technical data — standard web server logs (IP address, browser type) collected automatically by our hosting provider.

How we use it

Your captured text is sent to a third-party AI service to identify tasks, events, and notes within it. If you've connected Google Calendar, matching events are pushed to your calendar automatically. Your profile is used solely to make Loop's own suggestions more relevant to you — it is never used to advertise to you or shared for marketing purposes.

Who we share data with

We use a small number of service providers to run Loop. Each only receives the minimum data needed to do its job:

  • OpenRouter — processes the text of your captures to extract tasks/events/notes. Receives transcript text only.
  • Google — if you connect Google Calendar, used to create calendar events on your behalf via Google's Calendar API.
  • Supabase — hosts our database and handles login/authentication.
  • Resend — delivers the login code to your email address.
  • Render — hosts the application itself.

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers.

How long we keep it

Your data is kept as long as your account exists. You can request full deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing us (below) — we will delete it within a reasonable time.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. You can also disconnect Google Calendar at any time from the "About you" tab inside the app, which immediately revokes our ability to add events on your behalf.

Security

We use industry-standard practices to protect your data — encrypted connections (HTTPS), access controls on our database, and secrets that never reach your browser. No online service can guarantee absolute security, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Children's privacy

Loop is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of Loop after a change means you accept the updated policy.
